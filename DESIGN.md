# Design System: Wishrack

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Nani's Cardigan"**

The rack is a hand-knit navy cardigan you pull on at night. The ground is navy wool in three or four tonal plies; all text is oatmeal yarn; the only bright thing is a mustard button, and it appears only where a thumb can press. The logo is a horn cardigan button (four holes, stitched cross) and the signature is Fair Isle colourwork: price history is literally knitted, one stitch column per daily check, moss stitches where the price fell and berry where it rose. The same stitch knits a peerie band across the top of the save strip and a swatch on the passcode screen. Edges that meet other surfaces (under the bar, the top of the phone sheet) finish in a ribbed cuff stripe.

The density is cosy, not sparse: a 2-column photo-first grid on the phone, generous rounded panels, 44px touch targets everywhere, and a 15px body that is comfortable to read in bed. Night is the default because the owner scrolls in the dark; Day is not a white store app but a pale heather-blue jumper with navy as the ink. The world refuses the cold white store-app grid and the generic soft-card app; earlier kraft hang-tag and denim-patch looks are not part of this system.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Navy wool ground in tonal plies; depth comes from ply, not from glass or heavy shadow.
- Oatmeal yarn text in three strengths; never pure white, never pure black.
- Mustard reserved for pressable primary actions; berry for sale, price rises and delete; moss for price drops and saved.
- Bricolage Grotesque for the wordmark, headings and every price; Figtree for the UI; both self-hosted so the installed app keeps its type offline.
- Large soft radii (11 / 14 / 18px) and pill controls; identical 3:4 photo frames, tinted from the item's saved colour when there is no photo.
- Ribbed-cuff stripe edges and a single knitted stitch shape reused for the chart, the peerie band and the passcode swatch.

## Colors

A low-light navy-and-oatmeal palette with three small yarn colours that each carry exactly one meaning. Night values are the frontmatter's undecorated keys; Day values are the `day-` keys and replace them one for one under `:root[data-theme="light"]` and under `prefers-color-scheme: light` when no theme is set.

### Primary
- **Mustard Button** (`mustard`, hover `mustard-hi`, text on it `on-mustard`): the fill of primary actions: Save, Open my rack, Go to store, Save changes, the crop-handle knobs, the checkbox accent and the dashed border of the save strip while files are dragged over it. Its rarity is what makes the next step obvious.

### Secondary
- **Berry Yarn** (`berry`): sale percentage, price rises in the chart, validation messages, delete and lock buttons, error status.
- **Moss Yarn** (`moss`): price drops (grid flag, "Was" line, chart stitches), success status, the "on" settings dot.

### Tertiary
- **Horn** (`horn`): the logo button, the dash row of the peerie band and the gate swatch; also the "off" settings dot. A material colour, never a state.

### Neutral
- **Night Wool** (`wool`): page ground, the flag pill, the ring around crop handles, and (at 70%) the mask outside the crop box.
- **Raised Knit** (`wool-2`): bar, save strip, peerie band ground, review panel, sheet, select pills.
- **Rib Shadow** (`wool-3`): field and button borders, dividers between sheet sections, empty stitches, the dark rib of the cuff stripe, selected segment.
- **Worn Rib** (`wool-4`): hover borders, the sheet grab handle, link underlines at rest.
- **Deep Field** (`field`): inputs, the chart backing and the segmented control sit one ply deeper than the knit.
- **Oatmeal Yarn** (`yarn`, `yarn-2`, `yarn-3`): main text; secondary text, labels, captions and "no change" stitches; placeholders and tertiary notes. The peerie band's diamond row is `yarn` mixed 55% into `wool-2`, so it reads as a quiet ornament, not text.
- **Photo Ground** (`photo`): the empty 3:4 frame behind product photos and the crop editor.
- **Shade** (`shade`): the scrim behind an open sheet.

### Placeholder tints (data, not tokens)
A photo-less tile takes a muted tint of the colour the owner or the store saved for the item: the tint is mixed 42% into `photo`, and the store-name mark rises to 70% opacity. The tints are a fixed lookup by colour word (olive, taupe, black, grey, navy, blue, green, brown, red, pink, white, yellow, orange, purple); they come from saved data, are never invented, and are not available as UI colours.

### Named Rules
**The Mustard Button Rule.** Mustard fills only things that can be pressed, dragged or dropped onto. It is never a heading colour, a badge, a price or decoration. The two documented exceptions are text-interaction affordances: the text selection highlight (mustard with `on-mustard` text) and the input caret. Nothing else earns it.

**The Yarn Says Which Way Rule.** Moss means the price went down, berry means it went up or is on sale (or something will be destroyed). Every colour signal is doubled by shape: a down chevron on drops, a struck-through MRP beside the sale price, a check on bought items.

**The Heather, Not White Rule.** Day is a pale heather-blue jumper (`day-wool`), and the lightest surface in the system is the deep field (`day-field`). No surface is pure white and no text is pure black in either theme.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bricolage Grotesque (variable, weights 200-800, opsz; with Figtree, system-ui fallback)
**Body Font:** Figtree (variable, weights 300-900; with system-ui, Segoe UI, Roboto fallback)

Both faces are self-hosted as woff2 in latin and latin-ext subsets with `font-display: swap`; the latin files are preloaded and cached by the service worker so the installed app renders its own type offline. No third-party font request is made.

**Character:** Bricolage is the chunky, slightly hand-cut wordmark yarn: confident, warm, a little lumpy at 800. Figtree is the plain, friendly stitch that does the work underneath.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(32px, 9vw, 44px), 1.02, -0.025em, balanced): the passcode screen headline only.
- **Wordmark** (800, 24px, 1, -0.02em): "Wishrack" beside the horn button in the bar. **Wordmark gate** is the same lockup at 21px, pinned to the top-left of the passcode screen.
- **Placeholder mark** (800, clamp(22px, 6vw, 30px), 1, -0.02em, `yarn` at 55%, 70% on tinted tiles): the store name in a photo-less frame; 21px in the sheet hero's smaller frame.
- **Price big** (700, 26px, -0.01em, tabular): the price in the item sheet hero.
- **Empty headline** (700, 22px): "Your rack is empty".
- **Headline** (700, 20px, 1.15, -0.01em): panel headings ("Save something you saw", review heading).
- **Title** (700, 19px, 1.2, balanced): the product name in the item sheet hero; it is the sheet's heading.
- **Price** (700, 17px, 1.3, tabular): every grid price and the bar total.
- **Section** (700, 16px): sheet section headings ("Price over time", "Look for it elsewhere").
- **Sheet label** (Bricolage 700, 15px, `yarn-2`): the Settings sheet heading.
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.45): base text; settings lines run at 14px, capped at 60ch.
- **Passcode** (17px, 0.12em tracking): the passcode field only. **Input** (16px): every other field, so phones do not zoom.
- **Button** (600, 14px); **Button small** (600, 13px) for small buttons and chips; **Card title** (400, 14px, 1.3, two-line clamp).
- **Support** (13.5px, `yarn-2`, max 62ch): sub copy, status lines, counts, selects. **Hint** (13px, `yarn-2`): sheet hints, captions, link-ish text.
- **Label** (600, 12.5px, `yarn-2`): field labels, MRP and percent-off. **Meta** (400, 12px, `yarn-2`): store / colour / size lines, axis dates, legend.
- **Flag** (700, 12px, tabular): drop / bought pills on grid photos. **Flag small** (700, 11px) is the one size below 12px, used only on the sheet hero's smaller photo.

### Named Rules
**The Price Is Bricolage Rule.** Every price is set in Bricolage, right after the Rs. sign, with tabular figures. MRP and percent-off sit beside it in Figtree at 12.5px, the MRP struck through with a 1.5px line.

**The No Shouting Rule.** Nothing is uppercase and nothing is letter-spaced open except the passcode field (0.12em). Hierarchy comes from Bricolage weight and size, not caps.

## Layout

One centred column, max 1280px, with a 16px side gutter that grows to the device safe-area inset. Vertical rhythm is built from 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20px gaps, with 26px bands separating the bar, the save strip and the rack toolbar.

- **Phone (under 640px; sheets switch at 640px and below):** the rack is a 2-column grid (26px row gap, 10px column gap). Save-strip and review buttons stretch to share the row. Sheets rise from the bottom as a 92dvh sheet with a 22px top radius, a ribbed top edge and a 40 x 4px grab handle.
- **Laptop (above 640px):** the grid becomes auto-fill columns of at least 190px (32px row gap, 14px column gap), roughly 4 to 6 across. Buttons return to natural width. Sheets slide in from the right as a plain 460px drawer (no rib on its edge).
- **Review at 720px and up:** each read item lays out as crop editor (200-300px) beside its fields. The review scrolls into view clear of the sticky bar (84px scroll margin).
- **Save strip:** 30px top padding so the 16px peerie band sits clear above the heading.
- **Passcode screen:** a single 480px column centred vertically; the logo lockup is pinned top-left, the swatch (up to 360px) sits above the headline.
- **Forms:** two-column field grids (10px gaps); name, link and notes span both columns.
- **Bar:** sticky, with the wordmark left and the "N to buy" tally plus settings button right; the tally is right-aligned and tabular.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tonal ply first: ground (`wool`) is the deepest knit, raised panels and the bar are one ply up (`wool-2`), and fields sit one ply below the knit (`field`). Borders are 1.5px `wool-3` stitches rather than hairlines. Shadows are reserved for things that float over the knit: the sheet, the flag pill on photos, the bar's cuff and the primary button's soft glow. There is no blur, no glass and no backdrop-filter.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 40px -18px rgba(0, 0, 0, .7), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, .25)`; Day `0 18px 40px -20px rgba(27, 35, 64, .45), 0 2px 6px rgba(27, 35, 64, .12)`): the open sheet or drawer.
- **Cuff fall** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 10px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, .35)`): under the bar's ribbed edge, so the rack scrolls beneath it.
- **Button glow** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 14px -8px color-mix(in srgb, var(--mustard) 60%, transparent)`): a faint mustard warmth under primary buttons, following the theme's mustard.
- **Flag float** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 10px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, .5)`): the drop / bought pill sitting on a photo.
- **Crop mask** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 9999px color-mix(in srgb, var(--wool) 70%, transparent)`): darkens the screenshot outside the crop box in the theme's own wool.

### Named Rules
**The Knit, Not Glass Rule.** Surfaces are flat wool plies at rest. A shadow means the thing is floating over the knit (sheet, flag, bar); a panel inside the page never gets one.

## Shapes

Soft, generous corners in three main steps: 11px on buttons and fields, 14px on photo frames, the crop editor and the chart, 18px on the save strip and review panels. The phone sheet's top corners are 22px. Selects, chips, flags and the segmented control are full pills; the icon button, status dots, spinner, crop knobs and horn button are circles.

Three small radii are documented exceptions for thin or cutting parts, never for panels or controls: **tick** (2px) on the progress bar, the sheet grab handle and the chart legend keys; **cut** (4px) on the crop box, because it is a cutting frame; **line** (6px) on skeleton text bars and the rack card's text block focus area. The peerie band is square (0) because it runs edge to edge inside the save strip's clipped corners.

The recurring silhouette is the **ribbed cuff**: a 7px stripe of 4px `wool-2` and 2px `wool-3` ribs, repeated horizontally, hung under the bar and laid across the top of the phone sheet. The **horn button** (a 16px circle of `horn` with four `wool` holes and a darker inset rim) is the logo mark; the app icon is the same button on a ribbed navy field.

### Named Rules
**The Identical Frame Rule.** Every product photo is a 3:4 frame with a 14px radius and `photo` ground, cropped with object-fit cover. Missing photos keep the frame, take the saved-colour tint when there is one, and show the store name in Bricolage, never an invented image.

**The Ribbed Cuff Rule.** Where a surface meets the knit below it, it finishes in the 7px rib stripe, not a hairline or a hard drop shadow.

## Components

### Buttons
Tactile, rounded and plain: wool and yarn, with mustard only on the one thing to do next.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (11px), 1.5px border, 44px minimum height, 600 weight.
- **Primary:** mustard fill and border, `on-mustard` text, faint mustard glow; hover brightens to `mustard-hi`.
- **Outline (default / "ghost"):** transparent with a `wool-3` border and `yarn` text; hover shifts the border to `wool-4` and adds a 35% `wool-3` wash. Used for Choose screenshots, Add by hand, Check price now, Mark as bought, backup.
- **Danger:** berry text with a 45% berry border; hover fills berry at 10%. Delete asks for a second tap ("Tap again to delete") instead of a dialog.
- **Small:** still 44px high, with 13px text and 13px side padding, used inside the sheet.
- **Press / Focus / Disabled:** press nudges down 1px; focus is a 2px `yarn` outline at 2px offset; disabled drops to 45% opacity.
- **Icon button:** 44px circle, no border, `wool-3` wash on hover. Icons are inline 24-unit SVG strokes at 1.8px with round caps and joins.
- **Link-ish:** underlined `yarn` text with a `wool-4` underline that turns full colour on hover (Replace photo, crop actions).

### Chips
- **Style:** 44px pill outline, 1.5px `wool-3` border, 13px 600 text with a search icon; hover border goes to `yarn-2`. Used for "Look for it elsewhere" store searches.

### Cards / Containers
- **Rack card:** no box; a 3:4 photo frame, then meta (12px `yarn-2`), a two-line title (14px), and the Bricolage price. The photo links to the store; the text block opens the item sheet. Hover zooms the photo 3%.
- **Tinted placeholder:** a photo-less frame mixes the saved-colour tint 42% into `photo` and shows the store name in the placeholder mark.
- **Flag:** a `wool` pill on the photo's top-left with a 14px icon: moss "Rs. N drop" with a down chevron when the price fell, or yarn "Bought" with a check. Bought cards fade the photo to 40% with 60% greyscale and strike the title.
- **Save strip:** `wool-2`, 18px radius, clipped, with the peerie band knitted across its top edge and the heading below it. Dragging files over it turns its border to a 1.5px dashed mustard and warms the ground 20% toward mustard (a drop target is a control).
- **Review panel:** `wool-2`, 18px radius, 18px 16px padding, no shadow; items separated by 1.5px `wool-3` rules.
- **Item sheet:** `wool-2` with the lift shadow. The top row carries only the close button (right-aligned); the item name in the hero is the sheet's heading. The Settings sheet keeps a "Settings" label on the left of the same row. Sections are separated by 1.5px `wool-3` rules with 20px vertical padding.
- **Skeleton:** photo frames breathe between full and 55% opacity on a 1.6s alternate loop, with 12px `wool-2` text bars.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** `field` background, 1.5px `wool-3` border, 11px radius, 44px tall (48px for the paste-link field, 50px for the passcode), 16px text. Placeholders are `yarn-3`; the caret is mustard.
- **Focus:** the border shifts to `yarn-2`; no glow.
- **Warning:** a berry-tinted border with a 12px berry note under the field.
- **Select pills:** `wool-2` pills, 44px, 13.5px 600 text, a drawn chevron on the right.
- **Segmented theme switch:** a `field` pill holding Match phone / Night / Day; the checked option becomes a `wool-3` pill in full `yarn`.

### Navigation
The sticky bar is the only navigation: horn-button wordmark left, "N to buy" plus the Bricolage total and a settings icon button right, ribbed cuff below. Store and sort filters are select pills in the toolbar above the grid, with a tabular item count on the left.

### Crop Editor
The review shows the screenshot in a 9:16 `photo` frame; the crop box is a 2px `yarn` frame with a 4px corner and the crop mask outside it, with four 22px mustard knobs (3px `wool` ring, 46px touch area) sitting just inside each corner. While dragging, the frame turns dashed; keyboard focus shows a 2px mustard outline.

### Knitted Price Chart (signature)
Price history is a Fair Isle colourwork chart: each daily check is one column of six stitches, each stitch a "V" of two leaning loops on a 10-unit grid. The column's filled height is the price; filled stitches are moss when the price fell, berry when it rose and `yarn-2` when unchanged; the rest stay `wool-3`. The chart sits on a `field` backing with a 14px radius and 10px padding, is about 34px wide per column (shrinking to fit), keeps up to 24 checks, and is captioned with first and last dates, a small legend (only when a rise or fall is present) and a lowest / highest sentence. Columns knit in left to right on load (0.5s, 45ms stagger); the ornamental bands do not animate.

### Peerie Band and Gate Swatch
The same stitch knits two ornaments. The **peerie band** is a 3-row, 16px-high band running across the top of the save strip: a muted yarn diamond on `wool-3` stitches with a horn dash row, on `wool-2`. The **gate swatch** is a 26 x 7 band (up to 360px wide) on the passcode screen: a `yarn` diamond row and a `horn` dash row on `wool-2`, with a 14px radius.

### Named Rules
**The One Stitch Rule.** Any knitted graphic uses the single stitch shape on the 10-unit grid. New charts or ornaments are new colourings of that stitch, never a different drawing style.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep mustard (`mustard`) on pressable, draggable or droppable controls only (plus the text selection and caret), and give each view at most one or two mustard buttons.
- **Do** pair every colour signal with a shape: down chevron for drops, struck MRP for sale, check for bought.
- **Do** set every price in Bricolage with tabular figures, right after "Rs.", right-aligned where prices stack.
- **Do** use 3:4 frames with the 14px radius for every product photo, and the tinted store-name placeholder when there is no photo.
- **Do** build depth with wool plies (`wool` → `wool-2`, fields at `field`) and 1.5px `wool-3` borders.
- **Do** finish surface edges that meet the knit with the 7px ribbed cuff.
- **Do** keep every touch target at 44px or more (inputs, buttons including small ones, chips, selects, icon buttons, crop knobs via their 46px hit area).
- **Do** derive shadow and mask colours from tokens (`color-mix` with `mustard` or `wool`) so they follow the theme.
- **Do** change the Day theme in both places it is declared (the `data-theme="light"` block and the `prefers-color-scheme: light` block), keeping them identical.
- **Do** serve fonts from the app's own fonts folder and add any new font file to the service-worker cache.
- **Do** respect reduced motion: every animation and transition collapses to near zero.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use mustard for headings, badges, prices, borders at rest or decoration.
- **Don't** use pure white surfaces or pure black text in either theme; Day is heather-blue, not a white store grid.
- **Don't** add glass, blur or backdrop-filter, or shadows on in-page panels.
- **Don't** set text in uppercase or open letter-spacing for hierarchy.
- **Don't** use the 2 / 4 / 6px radii or the 11px type size outside the parts listed for them.
- **Don't** draw knitted graphics with any shape other than the shared stitch.
- **Don't** invent or generate product photos, or use the placeholder tints as UI colours.
- **Don't** bring back the kraft hang-tag or denim-patch looks; they are not part of this system.
